What Is The Score Machine?

A clearer way to understand and manage profile information.

The Score Machine is a credit-intelligence and workflow platform designed to help individuals and professionals organize, review, and monitor credit-profile information.

Instead of focusing only on a single score, the platform helps surface the broader information that may affect how a profile is evaluated — negative items, limiting factors, account information, utilization, report differences, inquiries, and other profile details.

The platform helps users organize this information into a more understandable experience so they can review their profile, track progress, use available self-service tools, and make better-informed decisions.
